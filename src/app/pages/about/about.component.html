About Kolkata Scotty

Transforming beginners into confident riders since 2014. We are committed to excellence in motorcycle training and safety education.

Our Story

Founded in 2014, Kolkata Scotty Bike Training has been the premier destination for quality motorcycle training in West Bengal. What started as a small training center with just two instructors has grown into one of the most trusted names in driver education.

Our mission is simple: to create safe, confident, and skilled riders through comprehensive training programs that emphasize both technical proficiency and road safety. We believe that proper training is the foundation of responsible riding.

Over the years, we've trained thousands of students, each with their unique goals and challenges. Our personalized approach ensures that every student receives the attention and guidance they need to succeed.

Mission
